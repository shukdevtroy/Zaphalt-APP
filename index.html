  1. Copy the Zaphalt APK to your phone (USB, Google Drive, WhatsApp) and tap it.
  2. If Android says your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unrecognized app, tap More details then Install anyway. It appears for any app outside the Play Store.
  4. Tap Open.

Tips for a healthier battery.

Keep it between 20% and 80% when you can. Use the full 100% only when you need it.

Dodge heat

Avoid charging under a pillow or in direct sun. Take thick cases off if the phone gets warm.

🎯

Aim at 80–90%

Set your alarm there. The wear comes from sitting at 100% for hours, not from reaching it.

🌙

Use Overnight mode

You won’t be woken up, and your battery won’t be parked at 100% all night.

Something not working?

Menu names differ between phone brands. Pick yours.

  • Raise the Alarm volume in your phone’s Sound settings. Media volume doesn’t affect it.
  • Make sure Do Not Disturb isn’t set to “Total silence”.
  • Tap Test alarm to check.
  • Open Settings › Apps › Zaphalt › Battery and choose Unrestricted, or tap Allow running in background inside Zaphalt.
  • On most phones, open Recent apps and lock Zaphalt (long-press its card, choose Lock).
  • Turn on Autostart for Zaphalt.
  • Set battery saver for Zaphalt to No restrictions.
  • Lock Zaphalt in Recent apps.
  • Remove Zaphalt from Sleeping apps and Deep sleeping apps.
  • Set Zaphalt’s battery use to Unrestricted.
  • Allow Auto-launch or Background activity for Zaphalt.
  • Set battery use to Unrestricted, then lock Zaphalt in Recent apps.
  • Install or enable a text-to-speech engine, for example Speech Services by Google, in your phone’s Text-to-speech settings, and download your language.
  • Check Voice announcement is on in Zaphalt’s Settings.
  • Remember the voice is off at your saved office. During sleep hours it needs Also speak while I sleep on.
  • Tap Allow on the yellow setup card, or enable notifications for Zaphalt in system settings.
  • If Current looks wrong in Battery diagnostics, tap Re-detect current unit & sign in Settings › Reliability.
  • “Estimating time…” that never goes away means your phone doesn’t report charging current.

Questions, answered.

Does my phone really overcharge?

No. Modern phones stop charging at 100%. The harm comes from staying at 100% for a long time and from heat, which is why Zaphalt lets you choose a lower level.

Does Zaphalt drain my battery?

It is built to be light. It wakes only when Android reports a battery change and loads the voice engine only when it needs to speak.

Does it work after a restart or with wireless chargers?

Yes to both. If Monitoring was on before the restart it comes back on, and any charging method Android reports as charging works.

Can I change the alarm sound?

Zaphalt uses your phone’s default alarm sound. Change it in your phone’s Sound settings.

Does it need internet?

Only on the Where screen, for the map picture, place search and directions. The alarm, monitoring, notifications and the radar work offline.

Android warns me when I install the APK.

That message appears for any app that is not from the Play Store. Allow installs from your browser or file manager, then tap More details and Install anyway on the Play Protect prompt.
